About us

Ducklings Studios

Ducklings Studios is a game development studio built around original ideas, collaborative production and long-term player communities. We want every project to have its own identity while maintaining the quality and ambition expected from a growing international studio. Our first project is The Final Banquet, a cooperative psychological horror game for 2–6 players.

The studio

Ducklings Studios is a game development studio focused on building memorable worlds, meaningful player experiences and ambitious projects designed to grow with their communities.

Vision

To build a lasting international studio where strong ideas, talented people and player communities can grow together.

Mission

We create original games with a clear identity, thoughtful design and a commitment to long-term quality.
