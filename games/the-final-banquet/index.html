The Final Banquet

A 2–6 player cooperative psychological horror experience set inside a Victorian manor where dinner is only the beginning.

About the game

The Final Banquet is a first-person cooperative psychological horror game for 2–6 players. Enter Blackwood Manor, prepare an impossible dinner, keep the fires alive and work together as the evening slowly reveals that your hosts may not be what they seem.
